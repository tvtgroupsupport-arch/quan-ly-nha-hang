# Nội dung trang giới thiệu trên Google Play (Store listing)

Dán từng mục vào **Play Console → Phát triển → Trang cửa hàng chính → Bản mô tả ứng dụng**.
Giới hạn ký tự của Google: tên 30 · mô tả ngắn 80 · mô tả đầy đủ 4000 · nội dung "có gì mới" 500.

```
Gọi món, bếp, thu ngân, VietQR cho nhà hàng — dùng được cả khi mất mạng
```

**App name:** `Restaurant Manager VN` _(hoặc giữ nguyên "Quản Lý Nhà Hàng")_

**Short description (≤ 80):**
```
Orders, kitchen, cashier and VietQR for restaurants — works offline
```

**Full description:**
```
Restaurant Manager is an all-in-one point-of-sale app for restaurants and cafés in Vietnam: table orders, kitchen display, cashier and sales reports on a phone or tablet.

• Works offline — data is stored on the device and syncs between the restaurant's devices when online
• Table & seat map, per-seat ordering, merge, move and clean tables, reservations
• Kitchen display by station with order tickets and chime
• Cashier with cash and VietQR bank-transfer codes generated inside the app (no Internet needed)
• Menu, recipes, stock in/out with automatic deduction, promotions
• Revenue reports, best sellers, Excel export
• Staff accounts with permissions, QR-code device pairing, activity log
• QR menu for guests at each seat — no app install needed
• Your data stays yours: stored on your device and in your own Supabase project, which the developer cannot access

14-day free trial, then monthly, 6-month or 12-month subscriptions through Google Play (cancel anytime). Support: tvtgroup.support@gmail.com
```
